# Rendering Fixture

Not a garden. This is the tree `garden-preview --fixture` renders: a vault whose only job is to put every element the theme styles onto as few pages as possible, so that a change to the stylesheet can be judged by looking at two screenshots instead of nine.

It is deliberately not the vault, and deliberately not the fixture in `checks/digital-garden.nix`. That one is scoped to the publish boundary and asserts on what it renders; this one asserts nothing and renders everything.

The three notes are filed in the vault's own folders — `_Slip_Box` and `_Reference/Lighting` — with one left at the root. That is not organisation for its own sake: the folder is where a note's topic comes from, the topic is what colours its foliage on the bonsai above, and a fixture whose notes all sat at the root would render a tree in one colour and prove nothing about the two the stylesheet declares. The note at the root is the third case, a note with no shelf and so no hue.

Which note goes where is decided the same way. `Every Element` is the longest and most linked, so it is the evergreen, and putting the biggest mass of foliage on a real topic is what makes the tree's main colour judgeable at a glance; the seedling — the smallest pad, six characters — is the one carrying the no-topic fallback, because that is the case that should be least of the picture.
