# A Seedling

A short note with no links, no sections and no substantial rewrites, which is
the lowest maturity the model records. It exists so the index shows all three
growth marks at once — seedling, sapling and evergreen — and it lives at the
vault root on purpose, so it carries no topic hue either: this is the neutral
mark, the thing to judge a mark with nothing to say against.

It is deliberately not linked from the landing page. A link would give it a
backlink, and a backlink would raise its score out of the seedling stage it
exists to show.
